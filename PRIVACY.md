# Privacy Policy — Apiary Field

**Last updated: 16 August 2026**

Apiary Field is a hive inspection logbook. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

## No account, no sign-up

The app does not have accounts. You are never asked for a name, an email address, or a password, and there is nothing to log in to.

## Your records stay on your device

Everything you enter — apiaries, hives, inspections, notes, photographs, and voice recordings — is stored only in the app's storage on your own device. None of it is uploaded, synchronised, backed up to a server, or transmitted anywhere by the app.

Because the records are stored only on your device, they are removed if you uninstall the app. Use the CSV export to keep a copy.

## Crash reports

The app uses Sentry (sentry.io) to report crashes and errors, so that problems can be found and fixed.

A crash report contains technical information about the failure: the type of error, where in the code it happened, the version of the app, the device model, and the operating system version.

Crash reports do not contain your inspection records, notes, photographs, or voice recordings.

## Sharing and export

Exporting a CSV file, or sharing a voice recording, is something you start yourself. The app hands the file to your device's standard share screen, and you choose where it goes — email, a messaging app, cloud storage, or a file on the device. The app is not involved after that, and does not keep a copy or a record of where you sent it.

## Permissions

The app asks for these permissions only when you first use the feature that needs them, and the app works without them if you decline:

- **Microphone** — to record voice notes during an inspection.
- **Speech recognition** (iPhone only) — to turn voice notes into text. This is done on the device.
- **Camera and photo library** — to attach photographs to an inspection.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising networks.
- No analytics or usage tracking.
- No location tracking.
- Your information is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
