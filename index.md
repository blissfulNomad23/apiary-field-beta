# Privacy Policy — Apiary Field

**Effective date: 22 August 2026**

Apiary Field is a mobile hive inspection logbook operated by **Denis Telenchenko**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Apiary Field stores, what limited information may be processed by third-party services, and the choices available to you.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests:

**Email:** flashinglights2154@gmail.com

## No account or sign-up

Apiary Field does not require an account.

You are not required to provide a name, email address, password, or other account information to use the app.

## Hive and inspection data

The information you create in Apiary Field, including:

- apiaries and hives
- inspection records
- notes
- hive settings and ordering
- voice recordings

is stored locally in the app's storage on your device.

Apiary Field does not operate a server that receives or stores this hive and inspection content.

Your hive records are not uploaded to an Apiary Field account or synchronised to an Apiary Field cloud service.

## Voice notes

If you record a voice note, the audio recording is stored locally on your device.

You may choose to share a recording using your device's standard sharing interface. In that case, you choose the destination and the file is provided to the app or service you select.

Apiary Field does not automatically upload voice recordings to its own servers.

## Speech recognition

Some versions of Apiary Field may offer speech-to-text functionality where supported by the device and operating system.

Speech-recognition functionality is provided through operating-system capabilities. Processing behavior may depend on your device, platform, language, and system settings.

Apiary Field does not operate its own speech-recognition server.

## CSV export and sharing

You may export your inspection data as a CSV file.

Exporting or sharing a file is initiated by you. Apiary Field passes the file to your device's standard sharing interface, where you choose what application or destination receives it.

Apiary Field does not keep a server-side copy of exported files.

## Crash and error reporting — Sentry

Apiary Field uses **Sentry** to identify crashes and technical errors.

When a crash or error occurs, Sentry may process technical information such as:

- app version
- device model
- operating system and version
- crash stack traces and error information
- technical identifiers and network metadata needed to deliver the crash report

Apiary Field is not intended to attach your hive records, inspection notes, or voice recordings to Sentry crash reports.

Apiary Field does not use Sentry Session Replay.

Sentry's privacy information is available at:

https://sentry.io/privacy/

## Purchases — RevenueCat

The iOS version of Apiary Field may offer a one-time in-app purchase to unlock additional hive capacity.

Purchase and entitlement handling is provided through the Apple App Store and **RevenueCat**.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to verify and maintain your purchase entitlement, such as:

- product identifiers
- purchase status
- transaction-related identifiers
- anonymous or technical identifiers associated with the app installation

RevenueCat does not need your hive records, inspection notes, or voice recordings to provide purchase functionality.

RevenueCat's privacy information is available at:

https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

## Advertising and analytics

Apiary Field does not display third-party advertising.

Apiary Field does not use advertising networks.

Apiary Field does not use behavioral advertising or cross-app tracking.

Apiary Field does not currently use a general-purpose product analytics service to track how you use individual app features.

## Location

Apiary Field does not collect precise real-time location information.

## Data sales

Apiary Field does not sell your personal information.

Apiary Field does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Data retention and deletion

Hive and inspection data remains on your device while it is stored by the app.

Records that the app allows you to delete can be removed from within Apiary Field.

Deleting the application and its local app data from your device removes locally stored Apiary Field records from that device.

Information processed separately by Sentry or RevenueCat is retained according to those services' own policies and legal obligations.

## Data stored by third parties

Apiary Field itself does not operate a server containing your hive and inspection records.

Limited technical information may nevertheless be processed by Sentry for crash reporting and by RevenueCat and Apple for in-app purchase functionality.

These third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their own privacy policies describe their data-processing and international-transfer practices.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about processing.

Because hive and inspection records are stored locally on your device rather than on an Apiary Field server, Apiary Field normally does not possess a server-side copy that can be returned or deleted on your behalf.

For questions concerning information associated with Sentry or RevenueCat processing, contact:

flashinglights2154@gmail.com

## Children

Apiary Field is not directed to children under 16.

The operator does not knowingly use the app to collect personal information from children for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Security

Apiary Field is designed so that hive and inspection content is primarily kept locally on your device rather than stored on an Apiary Field server.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device and for any files you choose to export or share.
