How the Application works Apiary Field is designed to work fully offline. All hive records, inspection notes, photos, and voice notes that you create are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage. The Service Provider does not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to this data. There is no user account and no login. The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence to process your data or generate any content. The Application does not collect precise real-time location data. The Application does not display advertising. What data leaves your device, and why Two third-party services are used for narrow, specific purposes. Neither has access to your hive or inspection data. RevenueCat — purchase processing If you unlock the paid tier of the Application, purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives information necessary to process and verify your in-app purchase through the Apple App Store — such as your anonymous App Store transaction identifier and purchase status. It does not receive your hive records, notes, or photos. RevenueCat's own privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy Sentry — crash and error reporting If the Application encounters a technical error, a crash report is sent to Sentry to help the Service Provider identify and fix bugs. A crash report may include the device model, operating system version, app version, and the technical details of the error (such as which part of the code failed). It does not include your hive records, notes, or photos. Sentry's own privacy policy: sentry.io/privacy Data the Application does not collect No account registration, no login No advertising identifiers, no ad tracking No analytics on how you use the app's features No cookies (the Application is a native mobile app, not a website) No automated decision-making or profiling No marketing communications — the Service Provider does not send email or push marketing of any kind International data transfers RevenueCat and Sentry may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Both companies provide contractual safeguards for international transfers (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as required by GDPR Chapter V. See their respective privacy policies linked above for details. Data retention Your hive and inspection data stays on your device for as long as you keep the Application installed, and is deleted when you uninstall it or use the in-app delete function. The Service Provider never receives a copy of this data. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are retained according to RevenueCat's own retention policy, generally for the duration needed to support purchase verification and Restore Purchases. Crash reports held by Sentry are retained according to Sentry's own retention policy, typically up to 90 days.
